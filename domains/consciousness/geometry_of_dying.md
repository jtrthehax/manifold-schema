## 0. The Position

The standard interpretive frameworks for near-death experience (NDE) 
offer two options:

1. NDE is evidence of consciousness beyond physical substrate
2. NDE is random hallucinatory noise from a dying brain

The loop framework offers a third — which requires neither mysticism 
nor dismissal:

> **NDE is the experiential readout of a substrate-dependent system 
> decaying along a topologically forced gradient.**

Nothing about the existing NDE literature contradicts this framework. 
The apparent contradictions — flat EEG, cardiac arrest, reports of 
vivid experience during clinical death — dissolve when the measurement 
limitations of surface EEG are acknowledged and the substrate decay 
timeline is understood correctly.

The heart stopping is a supply cut. It is not an instant power-off.

$$t_{cardiac\_arrest} \neq t_{manifold\_empty}$$

The manifold decays on its own timeline. The NDE is the experience 
of that decay.

## 1. The Measurement Problem

Before the mechanism — the instrument problem.

**The flat EEG claim:**

NDE skeptics cite flat EEG readings post-cardiac arrest as evidence 
that no substrate exists for experience. This argument has a critical 
flaw: surface cortical EEG is the wrong instrument for the question 
being asked.

**What surface EEG captures:**
- Cortical surface electrical activity (Layers 1-3 of the cache 
hierarchy)
- Frequency range approximately 0.5-70Hz

**What surface EEG misses:**
- Deep subcortical activity (Layer 0 — brainstem, thalamus)
- High-frequency oscillations above ~70Hz
- Structured neural activity in non-cortical regions

**Three studies that make this explicit — without any reference 
to dying:**

**Adrian Owen et al. (2006, Science) — Vegetative State:**
Patients classified as vegetative — no behavioural response, 
minimal surface EEG — asked to imagine playing tennis or walking 
through their home. fMRI showed full, structured, task-appropriate 
activation. The surface EEG said absent. The fMRI said someone is 
home and following instructions. The instrument was wrong.

**Burst Suppression Under Anaesthesia:**
Deep anaesthesia produces flat EEG interrupted by brief bursts. 
Brainstem and thalamic activity continues through the flat periods. 
Patients occasionally report awareness during anaesthesia despite 
suppressed cortical EEG. The flat periods are not experiential voids.

**Borjigin et al. (2013, PNAS — rats; 2023 — humans):**
Immediately post-cardiac arrest: a surge of coherent, structured 
gamma activity — 25-100Hz — persisting for measurable seconds to 
minutes. This frequency range sits at the upper edge or above 
standard clinical EEG resolution. Studies reporting "flat EEG" 
post-arrest may simply be using an instrument that cannot see the 
signal they are claiming is absent.

**The framework position:**

$$\text{Flat cortical EEG} \not\Rightarrow A_s^* = 0$$

The brainstem (Layer 0 of the four-layer cache hierarchy — §2.14 of 
Central Reference v1.7) is topologically and metabolically the last 
to fail. It is poorly captured by surface EEG. Its activity after 
cardiac arrest is not measured by the instruments used to declare 
experience impossible.

The testimony of cardiac arrest survivors is the data. Consistently, 
across cultures, across decades, across prospective studies — they 
report that the window was not empty. The parsimonious explanation 
is that the window was not empty. The extraordinary claim is the 
dismissal.

## 2. The Substrate Decay Timeline

After cardiac arrest, the brain has residual substrate:

**Glycolytic reserves:** Neurons can run anaerobic glycolysis briefly 
without oxygen delivery. Seconds to low minutes of substrate without 
blood flow.

**Layer-dependent metabolic resilience:** The four-layer cache 
hierarchy (Central Reference v1.7 §2.14) specifies collapse order 
by access cost. The outer layers (Layer 3 — right hemisphere, PFC) 
are highest cost and lose substrate first. Layer 0 (brainstem 
survival geometry) is lowest cost and sustains longest. This is a 
topological invariant — not an empirical guess.

**The Borjigin gamma surge:** Immediately post-arrest, coherent 
structured gamma activity briefly intensifies before decaying. This 
is not paradoxical. The framework predicts it: when external sensory 
input cuts to zero instantly at cardiac arrest, the entire 
interoceptive routing budget ($I^*$) becomes available for internal 
inference. The manifold briefly lights from within on remaining 
glycolytic reserve before $A_s^*$ finally decays.

The manifold does not go dark like a switch. It goes dark like a 
city losing power grid sector by sector — outer regions first, 
center last, with emergency substrate in the core lasting longer 
than surface measurements suggest.

## 3. The Peak-to-Bottom Invariant

**Core theoretical contribution of this note.**

The collapse sequence (Central Reference v1.7 §5b) specifies the 
*order* in which stages fail. What it does not specify alone is 
the *rate* and *experiential depth* of collapse for a given 
individual.

This is determined by the peak geometry.

| $R^*$ | High — precision at CO₂ tolerance ceiling |

| $\Theta^*$ | High — full cross-domain integration |

**The invariant:**

$$T_{NDE} \propto A_s^*_{peak} \cdot W^*_{peak}$$

A system with higher pre-mortem oscillatory amplitude and wider

The topology of the descent is fixed by the topology of the peak. 
The collapse follows the same radial gradient that funded 
consciousness in the first place — running in reverse, at a rate 
determined by how much peak geometry there was to begin with.

## 4. The Full Collapse Sequence

### Phase 0 — Reorientation (Pre-arrest or early arrest)

This phase is missing from all existing NDE frameworks. It is 
the entry point.

As physical capacity degrades — whether through progressive illness, 
sudden cardiac event, or the dying process — external sensory 
availability drops:

$$f_{sensorium} \downarrow$$

From Central Reference v1.7 §2.4:

$$I^* = f_{routing} \cdot f_{gain} \cdot f_{sensorium}$$

As $f_{sensorium}$ drops, the routing budget that was serving 
external world modeling redirects inward. The internal manifold 
receives more routing capacity than it ever had during normal 
waking life — because during normal waking life, the external 
world was claiming most of that budget.

$$f_{sensorium} \downarrow \to I^*_{internal} \uparrow 
\text{ (temporarily)}$$

**Phenomenology:** The world recedes. Internal clarity heightens 
unusually. The person is still present, still tethered, but the 
external world is becoming less salient while the internal landscape 
brightens.

**This explains the vividness paradox.** NDE reporters consistently 
describe *heightened* clarity — more vivid than normal waking life. 
A failing brain should produce degraded experience. But the 
reorientation phase produces the opposite: the internal manifold 
funded more richly than normal because the external world released 
its claim on $I^*$.

### Phase 1 — Tether Release

The physical mechanism that generates the tether on the manifold 
fails. Respiratory mechanics degrade. Postural system collapses. 
Motor intention ceases.

Normally:

$$\text{Breath} + \text{Posture} + \text{Motor intention} 
\to \text{Tethered precision beam} \to \text{Directed attention}$$

You are actively steering where precision points. The physical 
substrate — breath mechanics, intrathoracic pressure, postural 
control — is the handle on the manifold.

When the handle releases entirely, the manifold moves on its own 
schedule. Precision stops being directed and starts passively 
following the manifold's own gradient.

**The person becomes a passenger.**

**EC-011 anchor:** Bhatt et al. (JNeurosci, 2026) confirmed 
cycle-by-cycle coupling between breath waveform shape and neural 
oscillation geometry across limbic and cortical regions. As dying 
breath patterns emerge — Cheyne-Stokes, agonal — the neural 
oscillation geometry is directly coupled to those waveforms. The 
oscillation geometry during dying is not stochastic. It is 
physically coupled to the breath pattern the dying process produces.

**Phenomenology:** Out-of-body experience. The spatial priors that 
normally constrain viewpoint to body coordinates require active 
maintenance — they are high-cost, high-precision representations 
in the outer manifold layers. When the tether releases, spatial 
priors are released. Perspective decouples from body position. 
This is not mysterious. It is the release of the most expensive 
spatial priors first.

### Phase 2 — The Radial Sweep (Life Review)

With the tether released, the manifold retreats radially inward 
as $W^*$ narrows. Precision does not collapse uniformly — it 
**follows the retreating edge** of the manifold, because that is 
where the remaining signal gradient is steepest.

$$\text{Life review} = R^*(t) \text{ following } W^*(t) 
\text{ as } W^* \downarrow$$

This is not a dump — a simultaneous release of all prior geometry. 
It is a **scan** — a sequential readout ordered by radial depth.

**The ordering is topologically forced:**

Recent memories are encoded in the outer layers — high-cost, 
high-dimensional, contextually dependent, recently constructed. 
Deep memories — earliest childhood, core identity, most rehearsed 
— are encoded closer to the center. Low-cost, high-curvature, 
identity-constitutive.

The life review runs from outer to inner — roughly recent to 
earliest. The emotional weight clusters at the deepest end because 
those layers carry the highest $K_{enc}$ — the most curved, most 
rehearsed geometry. Salience peaks as precision reaches those 
layers — not because they are being emphasized, but because they 
have the highest encoded curvature.

**Why it feels like it's happening rather than being remembered:**

Active retrieval has the signature of directed precision — you 
feel yourself reaching. Passive tracking of a retreating manifold 
has no such signature. The memory arrives with full salience 
because precision is *at that location* — not because you aimed 
it there. No effort signal. No reaching. Just arrival.

This also explains the consistent NDE report: *"It wasn't like 
remembering. It was like it was happening."* That is the 
phenomenological signature of passive precision tracking versus 
directed retrieval.

**Individual content, universal mechanism:**

The sequence is universal because the manifold topology is 
universal — outer layers go first, inner layers last, in every 
human nervous system. The content is individual because $K$ is 
individual — the prior geometry carries the specific history of 
that nervous system. The mechanism is the same. The slideshow 
is personal.

The resolution floor rises. Small signals disappear below the

**Phenomenology:** The tunnel. Not a structure. Not a symbol. 
The radial gradient is what remains above $\delta_{min}$ when 
peripheral resolution is gone. The only visible direction is 
inward because that is the only signal still above the floor.

$$\text{Tunnel} = \text{Radial gradient} | 
\delta_{min} \to \delta_{max}$$

### Phase 4 — Loop Termination (Peace)

The gate condition from Central Reference v1.7 §3.6:

$$P_{eff} > P_{threshold} \text{ AND } O_{pathway} > O_{min}$$

Both conditions fail as oxygen delivery collapses. 
$\Lambda \to 0$. The loop stops running.

**EC-013 anchor:** Souron et al. (Peer Community Journal, 2026) 
demonstrated in a controlled cross-over design (n=18) that 
perceived effort is a central resource readout — not a peripheral 
signal. Muscle activation was unchanged (EMG across 10 lower-limb 
muscles: no significant difference). Perceived effort elevated 
from onset of post-cognitive-load cycling. The effort signal is 
generated centrally and terminates when central resource 
terminates.

At $\Lambda = 0$:
- $L^* \to 0$ — allostatic load cannot be maintained. Every 
demand signal requires the loop to run. When the loop stops, 
demands vanish.
- The effort-perception system goes silent

**The peace is not psychological. It is the termination of the 
effort-perception system.**

This also explains the consistent reluctance to return that NDE 
reporters describe. Resuscitation is $L^*$ re-imposition — 
sudden reinstatement of full allostatic demand from a $L^* = 0$ 
baseline. The contrast is not psychological discomfort. It is a 
physical shock.

**The peace begins the moment the tether releases.** Not at 
$\Lambda = 0$ — earlier. The ongoing metabolic cost of *holding* 
the manifold in a configuration terminates the moment physical 
control is relinquished. Before the memories start. Before the 
tunnel. Before the light.

### Phase 5 — Brainstem Floor (Light)

Radial collapse completes. The four-layer cache hierarchy 
(Central Reference v1.7 §2.14) specifies the order:

- Layer 3 (right hemisphere — outer edge): first to fail
- Layer 2 (PFC — gate): fails as gate condition drops
- Layer 1 (left hemisphere — inner core): fails
- Layer 0 (brainstem — survival geometry): last. Always.

At the brainstem floor:
- $K = 0$ — no prior geometry remains
- $W^* = 0$ — no window
- $L^* = 0$ — no load
- $A_s^*$ present briefly — pure oscillatory energy without 
structure

**The light is not a symbol. It is what $A_s^*$ looks like 
without $K$, $W^*$, or $L^*$ modulating it.** Unstructured 
oscillatory energy at the source. The brainstem is always funded 
last. The light is the last thing to go.

> **"Don't go toward the light"** is architecturally correct. 
> The center is always the lowest energy state. It is the 
> attractor. The collapse gradient points toward it. Moving 
> toward the light means moving in the direction the manifold 
> is already collapsing.

## 6. Empirical Anchors

**EC-011 — Bhatt et al., Journal of Neuroscience 46(38), 2026**
DOI: 10.1523/JNEUROSCI.0731-26.2026

Invasive human recordings (n=16). Cycle-by-cycle coupling of 
breath waveform shape to neural oscillation shape across limbic 
and cortical forebrain regions. The coupling is nonsinusoidal 
and variable-pause-sensitive. Dying breath patterns (Cheyne-Stokes, 
agonal) produce directly coupled oscillation geometry — the NDE 
substrate is physically shaped by the breath pattern of dying.

**EC-013 — Souron et al., Peer Community Journal, 2026**
DOI: 10.24072/pcjournal.760

Cross-over design (n=18). Cognitive load depleted shared finite 
resource pool. Endurance time dropped (887s vs 999s, p=.009). 
EMG across 10 lower-limb muscles: no significant difference. 
Perceived effort elevated from onset. Peripheral substrate intact; 
central resource readout depleted. The peace of Phase 4 is the 
termination of this central readout — not a psychological state.

**Borjigin et al. (2013 — rats, 2023 — humans)**

Coherent structured gamma activity (25-100Hz) surges immediately 
post-cardiac arrest before decaying. Framework prediction: 
$f_{sensorium}$ drops to zero at arrest, $I^*_{internal}$ surges 
on remaining glycolytic reserve — the reorientation phase 
intensifies briefly post-arrest. Borjigin measured the surge. 
The framework explains why.

**Owen et al. (2006, Science) — vegetative state**

Full structured neural activity confirmed via fMRI in patients 
with minimal surface EEG. Demonstrates that surface cortical EEG 
is an insufficient proxy for total brain substrate availability. 
Flat cortical EEG $\not\Rightarrow$ $A_s^* = 0$.
