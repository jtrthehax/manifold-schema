## What This Paper Argues

The brain is an energy budget allocation system operating on priors. 
The breath generates the budget. The geometry of the neural manifold — 
shaped by oscillatory amplitude, precision, and curvature — determines 
what operations are accessible at any moment. Every cognitive, emotional, 
perceptual, and social phenomenon is a specific configuration of the same 
geometric variables, all driven by breathing mechanics, all measurable via 
standard HRV instrumentation.

## Key Contributions

- Formalises the causal chain from breath to consciousness in a 
  single master equation with no new variables or instrumentation
- Anchors every variable in existing empirical literature across 
  independent research programs
- Demonstrates cross-domain perceptual confirmation: every major 
  sensory system degrades under load in the same geometric pattern
- Extends the framework structurally to the social scale — a society 
  under load collapses geometrically identically to an individual 
  nervous system
- Establishes fixed vs. stateful boundary conditions for clinical 
  states — resolving the mechanism behind FND, PTSD, ADHD, autism, 
  and depression as geometric configurations, not fixed traits
