## What This Framework Does

### Formalizes the Causal Chain
The complete pathway from breath to consciousness, with every arrow mechanistically specified—not described, not correlated, but causal.

### Anchors Every Variable in Existing Literature
Every term in the framework is independently confirmed across RSA, HRV, predictive coding, interoception, motor learning, and perceptual research programs.

### Demonstrates Cross-Domain Perceptual Confirmation
Every major sensory system—visual, auditory, semantic, interoceptive, social, motor, mnemonic—degrades under load in the **same geometric pattern**, drawn from independent literatures that were not designed to test this framework.

A society under load collapses geometrically identically to an individual nervous system—precision degrades, the prediction window narrows, outer regions lose access first, the system retreats to survival geometry.

### Establishes Fixed vs. Stateful Boundary Conditions
FND, PTSD, ADHD, autism, and depression are **geometric configurations, not fixed traits**. Every configuration is one breath away from a different configuration.

## For Researchers

### Minimum Viable Experiment

The field has interpreted HRV's statefulness as noise. The minimum viable experiment:

- Morning vs evening measurement, same subjects, same protocol
- Model $L^*(t)$ accumulation across the day
- Predict: $C_s$ follows a declining trajectory proportional to $L^*(t)$ accumulation

This requires no new instrument, no new metric, no new population. The range is the information.
